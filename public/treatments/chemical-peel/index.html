Chemical peels matched to brown skin

A controlled acid solution removes damaged surface layers and improves acne, marks, oiliness and texture. In brown skin, the right peel at the right strength, after skin preparation, is what prevents dark marks.

Written and reviewed by Dr. Sumya Pervin, FCPS (Skin & VD), BMDC A-59492 · Last reviewed October 2026.

Book a consultation See prices

At a glance

  • Sessions Usually 4–6, every 2–4 weeks.
  • Who performs Superficial: Dr or N · Targeted and body: Dr.
  • Downtime Superficial: mild flaking 2–3 days · Targeted: 4–7 days.

How it works

Skin preparation with prescribed creams for 2–4 weeks, a test spot if needed, then the peel is applied for a timed period and neutralised.

Is it safe for brown skin?

Yes, with preparation, conservative strengths and strict sun protection. We avoid deep peels on darker skin.

Before and after

No scrubbing or picking; gentle cleanser and moisturiser; sunscreen every 2–3 hours outdoors.

Possible side effects

Redness, stinging, dark marks (PIH) if the skin is not prepared or protected, rarely blistering.

Pricing

Prices for this treatment publish with procedure licensing. Your doctor will give you a written, itemised cost after assessment.
