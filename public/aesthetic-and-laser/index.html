Aesthetic dermatology, planned by a dermatologist

Aesthetic treatment on brown skin carries real risk of burns and dark marks in the wrong hands. Every plan here starts with a doctor’s assessment and a patch test where needed. Every price shows who performs the treatment.

LICENCE

Acne scars

Marks fade with time and the right creams; scars need procedures. Expect 40–70% improvement, not perfect skin.

Wrinkles & ageing skin

Daily sunscreen and a retinoid do the most. Microneedling and peels improve texture; injectables are not offered at launch.

Sun damage & pigmentation

Different spots need different tools. Diagnose melasma versus freckles versus post-acne marks first.

Unwanted hair

6–8 sessions reduce hair by most of its density; occasional maintenance is needed. Hormonal causes are checked first.

Hair thinning

Medical treatment comes first; PRP is an add-on, not a replacement for minoxidil.

Redness & visible vessels

Rosacea and steroid-induced redness need medical treatment first; vascular laser or IPL is a later option.

Medical facial

Chemical peels

A controlled acid solution matched to brown skin for acne, marks, oiliness and texture.

Microneedling

Fine sterile needles trigger new collagen — one of the safest scar treatments for brown skin.

PRP

Prepared from your own blood and injected into the scalp to support hair growth in pattern hair loss.

Laser hair removal
