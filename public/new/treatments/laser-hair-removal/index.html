Laser hair removal for brown skin — priced by area

A diode laser targets the hair root while sparing the skin around it. On brown skin, the right wavelength, cooling and settings make it both effective and safe.

How it works

Shave the evening before. We test-patch, set parameters for your skin type and record them every session.

Is it safe for brown skin?

Yes. Diode and long-pulsed Nd:YAG lasers are the preferred types for brown skin. We do not use IPL for hair removal on darker skin.

Is it permanent?

It is long-term hair reduction. Most patients have a large, lasting reduction, with fine regrowth that may need an occasional maintenance session. Hormonal conditions can cause regrowth, so we check for them.

Before and after

No sun exposure or waxing between sessions; sunscreen on treated areas.

Possible side effects

Temporary redness and bumps, rarely blistering or colour change. Every setting is logged and reviewed.
