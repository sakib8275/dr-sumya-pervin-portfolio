Before and after your procedure

How to prepare for a skin procedure, aftercare, what is normal, what is not, and how to reach us. Downloadable aftercare sheets.

Book a consultation See prices

Before: tell us if you

take blood thinners (aspirin, clopidogrel, warfarin, or newer anticoagulants; do not stop them without your prescribing doctor’s advice) · have diabetes, a pacemaker, or a history of keloids or cold sores · are pregnant or breastfeeding · have used isotretinoin in the last 6 months (for peels and lasers). Eat normally. Wear loose clothing. For facial procedures, come without make-up.

After: what is normal

Mild soreness, slight oozing for 24 hours, redness, small scabs, temporary darkening of the area.

After: contact us the same day if

bleeding that does not stop after 20 minutes of firm pressure · increasing pain, swelling, warmth or pus after day 2 · fever · blistering or burns after a laser or peel. Complication reviews within 7 days are free.

Possible complications (in plain words)

Infection, bleeding, a visible or raised scar (keloid), lighter or darker marks, rarely numbness. Your consent form lists those specific to your procedure.
