# Part 1 — Strategy on one page
## 1.1 The promise
**Specialist dermatology, diagnosis first — with prices you can see and a plan you can follow.**
Dhaka patients choose between two flawed options. The hospital OPD has sound medicine, but you wait two hours for six minutes. The beauty clinic has excellent hospitality, but no dermatologist is in the room and the price changes once you sit down. This practice is the third option: hospital-grade clinical judgement, delivered on time, explained fully, priced in the open and followed up.

|---|---|---|

| **Prices in the open** | A full price list with inclusions, who performs, and course totals. No “starting from”. | Hidden add-ons and bait pricing. |

## 1.3 Price position: upper-middle to premium, earned by inclusions
The price list sits at the upper-middle to high end of the Dhaka market, just below the Gulshan–Banani corporate tier on headline fees but above it on what the patient visibly receives. A higher price is justified only by what the patient can see on the page: doctor minutes, written plans, included follow-ups, disclosed operators, refunds and a price lock.
## 1.4 Voice rules
- **Plain English, short sentences.** Write for an educated non-doctor. Explain any medical term once, in brackets.
- **Numbers over adjectives.** “40 minutes with the doctor” beats “thorough”. “6–8 sessions, 4–6 weeks apart” beats “quick results”.
- **No superlatives or comparisons.** Never “best”, “No. 1”, “most advanced in Bangladesh”, or claims about other clinics.
- **No guarantees.** Use honest ranges: “most patients see a clear improvement by week 12”.
- **No colourism.** Never “fair”, “white”, “brightening”, “glow-up”. Use “even tone”, “pigmentation”, “dark patches”.
- **Every clinical page carries a byline and a review date:** “Written and reviewed by Dr. Sumya Pervin, FCPS (Skin & VD), BMDC A-59492 · Last reviewed [DATE].”
- **We say what we don’t do.** A page that says no earns more trust than a page that says yes.

# Part 4 — Behavioural design and choice architecture
The site uses well-established findings from behavioral economics to make good choices easier. It never pushes bigger spending. The test for every nudge: **would we be comfortable if the patient saw exactly why it is there?** If not, it is not used.
## 4.1 Principles built into pages and prices
| **Principle** | **How it shows up** | **Example on the site** | **Ethical guardrail** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **All-in pricing** (avoids “drip pricing”) | One price includes numbing cream, consumables, dressings and aftercare. | “Nothing hidden: consumables are inside the price.” | Lab tests are the only pass-through, at cost, with the receipt shown. |
| **Good–Better–Best** (compromise effect) | Three consultation depths; three bridal tiers; two Care Plans. | Comprehensive Assessment sits in the middle as “Best for long-standing concerns”. | “Recommended” labels are clinical guidance, never sales; the doctor may advise the cheaper option. |
| **Honest anchoring** | The real pay-as-you-go total sits next to the course or plan price. | “৳6,500 · Pay-as-you-go ৳8,400 · You keep ৳1,900” | Anchors are real single prices. No struck-through fake “was” prices. |
| **Bonus framing** | Courses framed as a free extra session rather than a % discount. | “6 sessions for the price of 5.” | Unused sessions refunded at the per-session price. |
| **Risk reversal** (loss aversion) | Refunds, price lock, fee credits, free complication review. | “Procedure Assessment ৳1,500 — fully adjusted against session 1.” | Refund rules are written in full on /terms. |
| **Zero-price effect** | Items that cost nothing are shown as ৳0, not hidden. | “Report review within 14 days — ৳0.” | Only genuinely free items. |
| **Mental accounting & payment pain** | 0% EMI shown monthly, total always beside it. | “৳4,167 / month · total stays ৳25,000.” | EMI never shown without the total; offered only for courses ≥ ৳15,000. |
| **Visible effort / operator disclosure** | Doctor minutes and “who performs” on every price. | Dr · Dr+N · N badges. | Badges must match the real roster; audited monthly. |
| **Authority** | Credentials, BMDC number, bylines, references. | Credential card under the hero. | Only verifiable qualifications. No “masterclass” padding. |
| **Costly signalling** | Publicly refusing lucrative but unscientific treatments. | “Not available here — at any price.” | The list must be honoured in practice. |
| **Ambiguity aversion** | Process, timelines, session counts and downtime spelled out. | 5-step consultation; “6–8 sessions, 4–6 weeks apart”. | Ranges, not promises. |
| **Choice-overload control** | Browse by concern first; at most 3–4 options per decision. | “What brings you here?” → 3 pillars. | No more than 4 tiers on any table. |
| **Defaults** | Reminders on by default; pay-per-session is the default payment. | Booking form: “Send me reminders” pre-ticked (can untick). | Default never adds cost. |
| **Present bias → implementation intentions** | Turn advice into a scheduled action. | “Pick a day. We’ll send your serial and what to bring.” Reminders tied to the prescription. | Patient can stop messages with one reply. |
| **Peak-end rule** | End every visit on a high: written plan, cost summary, check-in date. | Footer of the plan: “We will message you on [date].” | Promises made are kept (tracked on dashboard). |
| **Endowment** | Patient owns a plan and a photo timeline. | “Progress photo timeline you keep.” | Photos only with consent; patient can delete. |
| **Reciprocity** | Free tools, guides, community screening hour. | Mole check; printable aftercare sheets. | No email wall on tools. |
| **Social proof** | Verified Google reviews; real counts after 6 months. | “Rated [x]/5 from [n] Google reviews” (live widget). | No paid, invented or selectively edited reviews. |
| **Fresh-start timing** | Seasonal reminders at natural new starts. | Pre-wedding season (Sept), pre-monsoon fungal advice (May), New Year sun-care. | Educational first; no fake deadlines. |

** H1 ** Treatments we don’t offer, and why
** Body ** Some of the most profitable treatments in Dhaka’s skin market are ones we refuse to provide. Not because they are unpopular, but because the evidence doesn’t support them, or because they cause harm we see every week in clinic.
| **We don’t offer** | **Why (page copy)** |
|---|---|
| **Skin-whitening drips and injections** (glutathione and similar) | There is no good evidence they lighten skin safely or lastingly, products are often unregistered, and serious reactions have been reported. Skin colour is not a disease. |
| **Steroid creams without a diagnosis** | Over-the-counter steroid and “mixed” creams cause skin thinning, stubborn acne-like rashes, stretch marks, and make fungal infections spread. Many patients we see were harmed this way. |
| **“Instant fairness” or “glow” packages** | Lasting change in skin needs weeks. Packages promising instant results usually rely on bleaching agents or temporary effects. |
| **Treatment from a photo alone** | Skin diseases that look alike in a photo are treated very differently. We will advise you on what to do next, but we won’t prescribe without an examination. |
| **Unregistered injectables and products** | We only use medicines and devices registered with DGDA, bought through traceable channels. |
| **Guaranteed or permanent results** | Honest medicine gives ranges, not promises. Anyone guaranteeing a result is guessing. |

## 6.9 How the price list influences choice, and why it stays ethical
| **Device** | **Effect on the patient** |
|---|---|
| Three consultation depths with a highlighted middle | Most patients choose the middle option (compromise effect). The highlight is clinical: long-standing problems genuinely need 40 minutes. |
| “What you get” on every row | The patient compares value, not just price. It answers the market’s #1 complaint about hidden costs. |
| Who-performs badge + doctor minutes | Makes the premium visible, and stops the “saw the doctor for 2 minutes” bait-and-switch. |
| Defined bands instead of “from ৳…” | The patient can work out their own price. Uncertainty is the main reason people don’t book. |
| Real pay-as-you-go anchors next to plans | Shows savings honestly; no invented “was” prices. |
| “6 for 5”, “12 for 11”, “4th half price” | Bonus framing feels like a gift rather than a markdown, and protects the per-session price. |
| Fee credit, refunds, price lock, ৳0 items | Removes the fear of losing money, the strongest single barrier for a first-time premium buyer. |
| EMI shown monthly with the total | Reduces the pain of paying for larger courses while keeping the full cost visible. |

| **Clinical content (E-E-A-T)** | Byline with credentials and BMDC number on every clinical page; “last reviewed” date; 2–4 references to recognised guidelines (BAD, AAD, NICE, EADV, WHO); medical review every 12 months; no claims without a source. |

| **Performance** | Core Web Vitals “Good” on mobile: LCP < 2.5 s, INP < 200 ms, CLS < 0.1; self-hosted or preloaded fonts; lazy-load below-the-fold images. |
| **Privacy & data** | Consent for messages and photos; minimum data collected on booking; encrypted forms; patient right to access and delete; compliant with Bangladesh’s Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025 and aligned with GDPR principles. |
