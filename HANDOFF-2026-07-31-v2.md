### Why Turnstile instead of the planned WAF rule

**A WAF rate-limiting rule cannot protect the live site.** WAF rules operate on a *zone* you
control; `dr-sumya-pervin-portfolio.pages.dev` belongs to Cloudflare, so zone rules never see
that traffic. The T5 plan in `SUBAGENT-PLAYBOOK.md` silently assumed otherwise. A WAF rule
only becomes effective once `drsumyapervin.com` is connected, **and only for that hostname** —
the pages.dev URL stays reachable and unprotected afterward.

Turnstile is hostname-independent, so it closes the gap now. The WAF rule is still worth adding
later as a second layer.
