## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored for this product, not category-interchangeable.** The "Written Plan" world is load-bearing everywhere it matters for navigation: breadcrumbs read like a document path (Home / Medical dermatology / Eczema), every clinical page carries a byline and review date, cost notes state what is withheld and why ("Prices for this treatment publish when the laser suite opens"), the utility bar names the real chambers, and the turmeric "Consulting today" flag is driven by the same schedule module the booking API validates. A beauty clinic could not ship this nav, copy, or honesty pattern unchanged.

**Deterministic scan (Assessment B):** 898 raw CLI findings across 70 files; after DESIGN.md-filtering, the credible residue is small: 1 real skipped heading (tools/skin-check results render h1 → h4), ~88 advisory font-size off-ramp values (13.5px sits between the Label 12px and Body-small 14.5px tokens), the Menu button at 10px radius against the pill/rounded scale (65 pages), 1 admin dark-glow, 1 em-dash overuse. The big counts (cramped-padding 204, design-system-color 190, cream-palette 66, thin-border-wide-shadow 260) verified as false positives — wrapper-blind static matching and sanctioned tokens (Overlay shadow, paper ground, white-on-indigo). The live DOM scan added two real findings the CLI missed: `.pr-foot` bleeding to the viewport edge (136-char line, 0px from both edges) and long lines on `.pr-est-note` (~155ch) and leaf prose (~87–96ch). These agree with the design review's spacing findings.

**Visual overlays:** none user-visible — Assessment B injected the detector into its own headless browser tabs only; the findings above are the recorded console output of those injections.

## Overall Impression

The linkage architecture is genuinely well built — four redundant ways back (breadcrumb, full footer map, drawer that mirrors the mega menus, a 404 that keeps the whole shell), zero dead internal links out of 66 unique hrefs, and every leaf ending in the same confident diagnosis-first close. The problems that remain are precision problems: one hub-mapping rule sends six surgical treatment pages "back" to the wrong family, two finished pages have no door at all, the nav never tells you which section you're inside, and three spacing defects (tag/title collisions, unbounded price-page paragraphs, cramped mega rows) nick an otherwise disciplined system.

## What's Working

1. **Backward linkage is solved four ways.** Every content page has a Home/Hub/Page breadcrumb; the 404 reuses the full nav/footer/drawer; the drawer's expandable groups lead with "All conditions"-style hub rows; the footer carries the complete family map. A lost visitor cannot stay lost.
2. **Forward linkage points at the promise, not a product.** Every leaf ends in "Start with a diagnosis" (Book + What we don't offer), heroes pair Book/See prices, gated prices explain themselves instead of teasing, and hub pages list their complete families (aesthetic hub shows all 7 concerns and 9 treatments, including the "NOT OFFERED" body-contouring card).
3. **Spacing discipline is real, not accidental.** `--sec-y` and `--pad` clamp tokens, the 8/14/22/32 rhythm, sticky offsets that track header height exactly (78px desktop / 64px phone for the prices tabs), the 44px tap-target trick with matching negative margins, and a sticky bottom bar that pins in the thumb zone and never permanently covers footer content (verified live at scroll: it rests below the footer at document end).

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Six skin-surgery treatments breadcrumb back to the wrong family.**
`article.mjs` HUB() (and `stubBody` in build-pages.mjs) maps every `/treatments/*` page to "Aesthetic & laser". A patient who enters skin-biopsy, excision-surgery, electrosurgery, cryotherapy, intralesional-injection or skin-cancer-treatment from the **Skin Surgery** menu reads "Home / Aesthetic & laser / Skin biopsy" — the backward path contradicts the forward path they took.
*Why it matters:* breadcrumbs are the site's core location signal; a contradictory one erodes the trust the whole design is built on, and it misroutes exploration (a worried mole patient gets pushed toward aesthetic pages).
*Fix:* extend the mapping — surgical six → ["Skin Surgery", "/skin-surgery/"]; aesthetic treatments (medical-facial, chemical-peel, microneedling, prp-therapy, laser-hair-removal, pigment-laser, fractional-co2-laser, light-therapy) → Aesthetic & Laser. Same fix in `stubBody`.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable shape` then a content-module fix; verify with `/impeccable polish`.

**[P1] Two finished pages have no door: /skin-care-products/ and /community/.**
Both render full copy, both are in sitemap.xml, and **zero pages link to either** (verified across all 66 built pages). Humans can only reach them by URL.
*Why it matters:* orphan pages are unreachable trust content — the product shelf and the public-education work are exactly the "we say what we do" material this practice positions on; search will index them with no PageRank and no path in.
*Fix:* give them doors — Community fits the About mega ("Dr. Sumya" column) and the footer; Skin-care products fits the footer Visit column and /the-centre/ page. If they are meant to stay unpublished for now, exclude them from PAGES until their gate content is truly live — silent orphanage is the worst of both.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable shape` (IA decision) → small build change → `/impeccable polish`.

**[P2] The nav never marks the section you are in.**
No `aria-current`, no active styling; the top nav renders identically on every page. Breadcrumbs cover leaf pages, but on hub pages and Prices/Learn the only location cue is the H1.
*Why it matters:* recognition-vs-recall — the standard web convention (highlighted current section) is absent, so users re-orient from scratch on every page; screen-reader users get no landmark state either.
*Fix:* emit `aria-current="true"` on the matching nav item at build time (body[data-page] prefix match) and style it (indigo + underline or the existing hover state held).
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable polish` (small CSS + build change).

**[P2] Card tags collide with card titles (spacing defect, two hubs).**
`.card-tag` is absolutely positioned at top 16px / right 16px with no reserved space, so two-line titles run into the badge: on /learn/ ("Melasma is manageable, not curable" vs "4 MIN") and on /aesthetic-and-laser/ ("Sun damage & pigmentation" vs "PROCEDURE·LASER", "Redness & visible vessels" vs "FROM YEAR 2").
*Why it matters:* it reads as overlap on the two hub pages whose entire job is scanning cards; the badges carry gate information ("FROM YEAR 2") that must stay legible.
*Fix:* `.card h3 { padding-right: 72px }` where a tag exists (or move the tag into flow above the title).
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable layout`.

**[P2] Prices page paragraphs have no measure; one bleeds to the viewport edge.**
`.pr-foot` (centered footer line) and `.pr-est-note` have no max-width — live-verified 188ch and ~155ch lines, and `.pr-foot` rendering 0px from both edges at 1366px. Leaf prose also drifts over the 76ch target (~87–96ch on condition pages' wide elements).
*Why it matters:* the 76ch measure is a stated system rule; 180-character lines are measurably harder to read on the site's most decision-dense page.
*Fix:* `max-width: 70ch` (+ `margin-inline: auto` for the centered `.pr-foot`), and audit `.s-prose` children for full-bleed elements.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable layout`.

**Minor observations**
- Mega-menu rows are ~25px tall (`padding: 4px 0`) — below the site's own 44px standard for touch-laptop users; the mega-foot arrow wraps onto its own line ("Confidential sexual health" / "→").
- Mega menus omit three pages their hubs list: fractional-co2-laser, light-therapy, body-contouring. Defensible curation, but body-contouring ("NOT OFFERED") is an ethical differentiator worth a mega feature slot; the Year-2 treatments could group under a small "From 2027" head.
- /treatments/laser-hair-removal/'s Related block is thin ("Unwanted hair · Prices") and misses its own companion article /learn/laser-hair-removal-permanent/.
- The utility bar's "Consulting now at Alliance Hospital and DCIMCH" is static and day-blind — on Fridays (both chambers closed) and Thursdays (DCIMCH closed) it contradicts the schedule-honest flags below it. Soften the copy or drive it from schedule.js like the home flags.
- Learn hub group head "Pigmentation & pigment" reads oddly next to "Skin, hair & nails".
- Detector residue worth one line each: tools/skin-check results skip h2/h3 (h1 → h4); Menu button is 10px-radius against the pill scale; a handful of 13.5px sizes sit between tokens.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (confused first-timer):** The condition journey works — "What brings you here?" chips, the skin-check feature in the Conditions menu, plain labels. Red flags: (1) landing on /treatments/light-therapy/ from eczema's "Phototherapy" link, the breadcrumb says "Aesthetic & laser" for what felt like medical treatment (the P1 hub-mapping bug); (2) on gated treatment pages, "Prices publish when the laser suite opens" needs a second read to understand you can still be seen and priced today — the follow-on sentence saves it, barely.

**Casey (distracted mobile user):** The sticky Call/WhatsApp/Book bar pins in the thumb zone at all times (verified at scroll), drawer rows are 48px, price tables become labelled stacked cards. Red flags: three sticky chrome zones on a phone (utility bar, header, prices tabs, plus the bottom bar) leave ~164px of fixed chrome on an 844px screen on /prices/ — dense; and the mega→drawer parity means the drawer's Care groups inherit the same three-page omissions.

**Riley (stress tester):** Direct load of any deep URL gets correct breadcrumbs and a complete footer; zero dead links across 66 pages; the 404 is exemplary. Riley does find: /concerns/body-contouring/ has exactly one inbound path (aesthetic hub) — fragile; and the utility bar's "Consulting now" claim contradicts the chamber schedule two sections below on closed days.

**Farhana, 29 (project persona — melasma, phone-only, English second language, deciding with her mother):** Home pillar "Look like yourself, rested" → melasma condition page → Related skin-type guide → Book is a clean, reassuring path; the cost note honestly separates Centre-2027 prices from today's hospital tariffs. Red flag: none of it exists in Bangla yet (by owner rule — gated on her approval), so Farhana's mother must rely on her translation at exactly the decision moment the site is designed for.

## Questions to Consider

- What if every treatment leaf ended with an explicit triangle — its condition, its price row, and its booking depth — instead of a one-line Related list?
- Should the nav know where you are? One held indigo marker closes the last recognition gap the breadcrumbs can't.
- Are Community and Skin-care products content you want found now? If yes they need doors; if not, the gate should hide them — silence is the one state that serves nobody.
