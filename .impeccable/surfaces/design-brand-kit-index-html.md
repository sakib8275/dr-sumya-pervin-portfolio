# Brand kit (design/brand-kit/)

Scope: extension of the established Nil & Haldi world and the approved Signature SP mark. Mode: Read (guidelines page) plus print and social assets. Audience: the owner's designer, printers in Dhaka, practice staff; downstream, patients who receive the cards, plan pad and WhatsApp images. Owner delegated all decisions; no question round was held.

Constraints: mark geometry unchanged; one colour; turmeric at most once per view, never inside the mark; Outfit only; no photos of the doctor; no claims beyond confirmed facts (title, specialty, qualifications from content/source/website-content.md; hours from functions/lib/schedule.js). Everything generated by scripts/render-brand-kit.mjs.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The kit is the practice's "Written Plan" in paper form. Refuses the generic brand-book of mockup photos and gradient hero cards.
OWN-WORLD: Deep Nil floods, paper/white stock, indigo ink, 2px ink rules over open columns, tabular figures for hours, one turmeric rule or underline per piece.
STORY: A printer or staff member finds the right file, sees how it is used, and downloads it; a patient leaves with a plan pad sheet and follow-up card that match the website.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline "One mark, drawn once, used everywhere." left with turmeric underline; the mark on a Deep Nil plate right, drawn in on load (reduced-motion static).
FORM: Extension of an established world; no concept roll (no seed key).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
