# A11Y-AUDIT-2026-10-28 — WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility audit (round 5)

**Scope:** the patient-facing site (`content/` → `scripts/build-pages.mjs` →
`public/`, plus `public/css/site.css` and `public/js/site.js`) and the staff-only
admin console (`public/admin/index.html`, `public/js/admin.js`,
`public/js/cms.js`, `public/css/style.css`). Target: WCAG 2.2 level AA.

**Method:** static reading of the sources and the generated pages only. No
browser, test suite or scanner was run, and no file other than this one was
changed. Line references are to commit `1ffbdbf`. The two High findings on the
patient site follow directly from the CSS; how often focus actually ends up
hidden (finding 2) depends on the browser and should be confirmed at 390px.

**Already solid (do not regress):** skip link to a focusable `<main>`; the mobile
drawer traps Tab, closes on Escape and returns focus to the Menu button; the
package detail is a native `<dialog>`; the booking form marks errors per field
(`aria-invalid` + `aria-describedby`), shows a summary that takes focus, and sets
`autocomplete`; reduced motion covers every animation; fonts are in `rem`; tap
targets are ≥44px; `tests/contrast.test.mjs` pins the colour tokens. Pairings
that test does not cover were computed by hand and pass (`--ink-2` on
`--nil-tint` 6.0:1, white on `--haldi-ink` 5.9:1, `--field-line` on white 3.7:1).

## Findings

Ranked most severe first. "Admin" rows affect only staff using `/admin/`.

| # | Category | WCAG criterion | Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Keyboard / ARIA | 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value; 1.4.13 Content on Hover or Focus | The desktop dropdown menus open whenever focus is inside them (`site.css:221`, `:226-227`), not only when the button is pressed. `aria-expanded` therefore reads "false" while a menu is visible. Pressing the button again, or Escape (`site.js:22-27`), returns focus to the button, which keeps the menu open: it cannot be dismissed. Keyboard users also tab through ~40 submenu links on every page to get past the header. | High | Drop the `:focus-within` selectors so the `.open` class (set by the button) alone controls the menu; close it when focus leaves the dropdown. |
| 2 | Keyboard | 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum) | No `scroll-padding` exists, but the header is sticky (64–78px, `site.css:183-184`), the `/prices/` section tabs are sticky (`:857`) and the mobile Call/WhatsApp/Book bar is sticky (`:1021-1026`). At ≤820px (phones, or desktop at 200% zoom) a focused link can sit entirely under a bar; the browser counts it as in view and does not scroll. Price-tab jumps and the hero's `#now` link also land under the header. Only `.tool-out` has a scroll margin (`:424`). | High | `html { scroll-padding-block: 78px 0 }`; at ≤820px `64px calc(56px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`; add the tab-bar height on `body[data-page="/prices/"]`. |
| 3 | Keyboard (admin) | 2.1.1 Keyboard | The photo upload area is a plain `div` that only responds to clicks, with the file input `display:none` (`admin/index.html:79-84`, `cms.js:284`). "Import Backup JSON" is a `<label>` around another `display:none` file input (`admin/index.html:217-220`). Neither works without a mouse. | High | Keep the file inputs focusable but visually hidden (`.vh`-style), or make the upload area a real `<button>` that calls `photoFileInput.click()`. |
| 4 | Contrast (admin) | 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) | The "WhatsApp Patient" button is white on `#25D366`, 1.98:1 (`style.css:280-283`, rendered at `cms.js:851`). | High | Dark text on the green, or a darker green such as `#128C4A` (≥4.5:1 with white). |
| 5 | Motion | 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide | On desktop the hero carousel advances every 2.4s and never stops by itself (`site.js:39-49`, `:165`; `site.css:522`). It pauses on hover or keyboard focus; the only permanent stop is pressing an arrow or a slide button, which nothing tells the visitor. Screen-reader users reading without moving focus have content change under them. Reduced-motion and touch visitors never get autoplay. **Owner decision:** the Pause control was removed at Dr. Sumya's direction on 2026-10-06 (`STATUS.md`), with the manual step documented as the 2.2.2 stop. This row records the conformance risk of that choice; it is not an oversight to fix without her. | Medium | If the owner agrees: restore a visible Pause/Play button as the carousel's first control (WAI-ARIA APG carousel pattern) and lengthen the dwell. |
| 6 | Forms | 3.3.1 Error Identification | On the skin-type guide and the visit-prep check, a missed question only produces "Please answer all six questions" below the form (`site.js:471-474`, `:726-729`). The missed question is not identified. | Medium | Name the unanswered questions, set `aria-invalid` on their fieldsets, and move focus to the first one. |
| 7 | ARIA (admin) | 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value | The selected admin tab is shown only by a CSS class (`admin/index.html:56-63`, `cms.js:265-274`). | Medium | Tabs pattern (`role="tablist"`/`tab`/`tabpanel`, `aria-selected`, arrow keys), or at minimum `aria-pressed`/`aria-current` on the buttons. |
| 8 | Status messages (admin) | 4.1.3 Status Messages; 3.3.1 Error Identification | Login and 2FA errors (`#pinError`, `#twofaError`, `#twofaSetupError` at `admin/index.html:30`, `:44`, `:158`; set in `cms.js:163-237`) are plain `div`s made visible but never announced, and are not tied to their inputs. | Medium | `role="alert"` on each, plus `aria-describedby` and `aria-invalid` on the inputs. |
| 9 | Focus (admin) | 2.4.3 Focus Order; 4.1.3 Status Messages | "Update Status" and "Delete" redraw the whole appointment list with `innerHTML` (`cms.js:826`, called from `:861-883`), destroying the focused button; focus falls to the top of the page. A successful status change is not announced. | Medium | After redrawing, refocus the same row's button (or the next row's); call `cmsToast` on success. |
| 10 | Non-text contrast (admin) | 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast | Admin text fields share the panel's background and are outlined only by `--hair` at 1.43:1 (`style.css:1251-1257`). | Medium | Use `--field-line` (3.7:1), as the patient booking form does. |
| 11 | Screen reader | 2.4.6 Headings and Labels | Every package card's button is named "See what's included" (`pkgview.mjs:55`): three identical buttons on the home page, more on `/prices/`. | Low | Append visually hidden text naming the package after the visible text (keeps 2.5.3 Label in Name). |
| 12 | Screen reader | 1.3.1 Info and Relationships | Once the script runs, the carousel's `<li>` slides get `role="group"` inside the `<ol>` (`site.js:69-73`, markup `home.mjs:126-133`), breaking the list structure. | Low | Put the group role on an inner wrapper, or render `div`s instead of `<ol>`/`<li>` when the carousel goes live. |
| 13 | Forms | 4.1.3 Status Messages | On `/book/`, the closed-day/past-date error appears as soon as the date changes (`site.js:601`) but its error area (`book.mjs:22`) is not announced until submit. | Low | `aria-live="polite"` on the `.bk-err` areas. |
| 14 | Screen reader | 1.3.1 Info and Relationships | The mole, skin-type and prep tools put their results under an `h3` directly after the page `h1` (`site.js:458`, `:472`, `:727`). The skin check already inserts a hidden `h2` (`:504`). | Low | Reuse the skin check's hidden `h2` in the other three tools. |
| 15 | Screen reader | 1.3.1 Info and Relationships | At ≤820px the price table hides its header row with `display:none` and blocks its rows (`site.css:1070-1071`). Safari/VoiceOver drops table structure; "Service / Price / Who" survive only as CSS-generated text. | Low | Hide the header row with `.vh` instead, or add explicit table roles. |
| 16 | Landmarks | 1.3.1 (best practice) | The top contact bar and the mobile action bar sit outside any landmark (`build-pages.mjs:95`, `:148`). Breadcrumbs are a `<p>` rather than `<nav aria-label="Breadcrumb">` (`article.mjs:89`). The "Dr"/"N" price badges explain themselves only through `title` (`prices.mjs:8`); the legend mitigates. | Low | Wrap in suitable landmarks; use a breadcrumb `<nav>`; add visually hidden expansions to the badges. |
| 17 | Admin misc. | 1.3.5 Identify Input Purpose; 2.4.6 Headings and Labels | The login PIN field is `autocomplete="new-password"` (`admin/index.html:28`), which discourages password managers from filling the saved PIN. Tab and button labels start with emoji (read aloud as "clipboard", "lock", …). The page has no `h1`. | Low | `current-password`; wrap emoji in `aria-hidden` spans; add an `h1`. |

## Verification and tooling

- Finding 1: tab to "Conditions" in Chrome at ≥1101px; the menu opens on focus
  with `aria-expanded="false"` and stays open after Escape.
- Finding 2: at 390px, tab down any long page and watch for focus under the
  Call/WhatsApp/Book bar; repeat with Shift+Tab on `/prices/` under the tabs.
- For ongoing coverage, `@axe-core/playwright` in the existing e2e suite would
  catch findings 12, 15 and the contrast rows automatically.
