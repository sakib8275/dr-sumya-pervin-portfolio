### Findings

- **Nit:** `scripts/render-brand-kit.mjs:69-74` — `area: 'Shyamoli'` is
  hardcoded for every chamber after the `CHAMBERS_NOW.map`. It is true
  today (both chambers' addresses contain Shyamoli,
  `content/site.mjs:52-58`) but will silently mislabel any future chamber
  outside Shyamoli. Fix: derive it from the chamber address or add an
  explicit `area` field to `CHAMBER_PLACES`.

- **Nit:** `scripts/render-brand-kit.mjs:243` — "Where to see Dr. Sumya
  **this week**" on a static PNG. It self-expires; fine if the owner
  regenerates weekly, otherwise drop the word "week". Same class:
  "Bring this card back with you." (`:403`) and "For a serial, call or
  WhatsApp" (`:432`) are instructions rather than facts — acceptable, but
  they are the only printed strings without a source.

- **Nit:** quals line omits the years and "BCPS" that
  `website-content.md:4` treats as confirmed (`MBBS (SSMC, 2011) · DDV
  (BSMMU, 2019) · FCPS — Skin & VD (BCPS, 2025)`). The kit matches the
  site's own credential line instead, so this is consistent — confirm that
  is the intended canonical form.

- **Should-fix:** regeneration byte-dirties the tree (8 files here): PDFs
  embed Chromium's `CreationDate` and preview PNGs drift by sub-pixel
  anti-aliasing. Every rerun will dirty the PR/review diff. Fix options:
  post-process the PDF metadata date (`/CreationDate` zeroing), accept the
  churn, or compare preview PNGs perceptually in CI. Until then, a
  regeneration is a real commit, not a no-op.

- **Nit:** 5.4–6.6 pt card labels (`scripts/render-brand-kit.mjs:367,380,385-386`)
  are at/below the practical print floor (~6–7 pt) on 89×51 mm cards —
  likely readable on coated stock at close range, worth a proof check.
